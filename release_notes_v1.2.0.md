## JustATuner v1.2.0

- Chord progressions. The drone can move through a progression on its own while you play over it: in bars at a tempo, in seconds, or one chord each time you press a key. Pick a preset or type your own, like `C | F | G7:2 | Am`. **Drone > Progression…**
- Eleven chords instead of four, all as just ratios, and chord changes no longer click. **Drone > Voicing**
- Speakers or headphones. With **Speakers**, the app listens to the room for a few seconds when the drone starts, then takes the drone out of what the mic hears, so it reads you and not itself. Stay quiet while it listens. **Exerciser Options > Monitoring**
- The tuner reads low notes right. An in-tune note could read up to 15 cents off at the bottom of the range.
- A sharp note drifts right and a flat note drifts left on every wheel. Some rows ran backwards.
- The strobe runs at the frame rate you set. On Windows, 60 was really about 32.
- The discs look solid. Room noise no longer moves the inner rings.
- Nothing gets cut off on a laptop screen.
- A microphone plugged in after the app started is found. **Tuner > Settings…** looks when it opens; on the drone tab, **Exerciser Options > Input > Refresh Devices**.
- The tuner no longer crashes on a high-resolution laptop display.
- The Linux download is built for Ubuntu 24.04 and newer.
- Bug fixes.

I don't own a Mac, so the Mac version is checked by automated tests only. If something is off, **Help > Open Log File** and send it to me.

Install steps for each platform are in the [README](https://github.com/stohrermusic/justatuner#installation).
